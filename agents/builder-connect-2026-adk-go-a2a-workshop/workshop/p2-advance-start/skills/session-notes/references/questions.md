# Good questions for a speaker

- What surprised you most when you put this into production?
- What would you do differently if you started again today?
- Where does this approach stop working: what scale, data or team size?
- How do you test or evaluate it?
- What does it cost to run, and what drove that cost down?
- Which part would you open source, or have you already?
- What should someone try first, on Monday morning, to get started?
