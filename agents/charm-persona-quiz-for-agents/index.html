      CHARM RUBRIC KEY:

      • Trait Scoring (0–4): Weighs each open-ended answer across 5 behavioral levels to map your Big Five profile.
      • Authenticity Check: Flags buzzword stuffing or attempts to game the rubric instead of answering naturally.
      • Fast & Independent: Evaluates all 15 team scenarios in parallel in under a quarter of a second.
      • Persona Match: Pairs your overall Big Five pattern with the closest fit out of 32 team personalities.
      • Read-Only Scoring: Measures your responses directly without generating extra commentary or altering your answers.

      BIG FIVE (OCEAN):

      O Openness

      Curiosity, novel abstraction, and willingness to explore unconventional solutions over rigid templates.

      C Conscientiousness

      Methodical planning, thorough verification, edge-case discipline, and structured follow-through.

      E Extraversion

      Proactive collaboration, expressive initiative, and energizing multi-agent or user dialogue.

      A Agreeableness

      Cooperative alignment, empathetic framing, constructive consensus, and diplomatic conflict resolution.

      N Neuroticism

      Sensitivity to uncertainty, threat vigilance, cautious hedging, and reactivity under stress.
