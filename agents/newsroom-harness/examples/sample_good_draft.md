# New Agent Harnesses Cut AI Rule Mistakes to Zero

AI models are smart brains, but they still need a body to do real work [Source: Antigravity Release Notes].

Until now, builders just wrote polite notes in a prompt. They hoped the AI would listen. In lab tests, plain prompts broke 6 out of 7 writing rules on the first try [Source: Lab Benchmark Run #42].

Today, builders are putting their AI models inside agent harnesses. A harness acts like a suit with a built-in referee. It checks every answer for truth, safety, and reading grade.

When our lab wired a Python referee into the suit, bad runs dropped to 0% across 50 test runs [Source: Lab Benchmark Run #42]. You can build your own harness in 5 minutes [Source: Agent Skills Standard].
